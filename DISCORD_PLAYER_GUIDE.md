# Play Raphael's Council in Discord

*Player-facing copy to pin alongside the deck once the Discord bot is connected.*

Welcome to **The Lanterns of Greyharbor**. You belong to the Lantern Compact: a small company asked to go where a city's promises no longer reach. Raphael watches over the chosen heroes. He offers guidance, but your choices remain your own.

You play with buttons and ordinary words. You do not need commands, dice formulas, or another app.

## Join the company

Open the pinned **Last Hearth** card and press **Join the company**. Give your hero a name and describe their talents, one important bond, and why they answer the call.

For example: “Maren is a ranger who once went back for a stranger. She is patient, fears forgetting the people she loves, and owes Lysa a promise.”

Review the character draft under **My hero**. Ask for changes with **Describe my hero**, then press **Approve hero draft** when the sheet is right. Once the host adds you to today's roster, press **I'm ready**. Use **Sit this session out** if you will not be playing.

## Bring an existing character PDF

If the host has enabled Raphael's character desk, press **My Hero → Import PDF**.
Upload your exported or scanned character PDF and type **2024** to confirm the rules
edition. Use **Review Import** and **Refresh** to check local OCR progress.

Review each page with **Previous/Next**. **Correct Details** accepts ordinary words
such as “My Dexterity is 16.” Uncertain or conflicting readings must be confirmed or
corrected before **Approve Import** saves the character. **Cancel** leaves your
existing character unchanged. Your PDF and review are not posted to the party.

When your sheet changes, use **Upload Updated PDF** and approve the displayed
differences. This does not restore spent spell slots, HP, consumables, or erase
campaign history. Reopen the saved character with **My Hero → View Character**.

The character desk is a working importer; the adventure buttons described below
also require the host's game-engine integration.

## Choose what matters

Press **Missions** to read the requests waiting at the Last Hearth. Reading a briefing does not accept the job.

To offer a job to the group, press **Propose this mission**. Everyone responds with **Support plan** or **Object**. Use **Discuss or suggest changes** to offer another idea. The plan moves forward only after the whole participating company agrees and someone presses **Confirm agreed plan**.

## Play a scene

Read what your hero can observe, then choose:

| Button | Use it to… |
| --- | --- |
| **Do something else** | Try any approach you can describe. |
| **Speak** | Say something to a character or ask them a question. |
| **Look closer** | Examine a person, place, or object. |
| **Show what I see** | Request an image of your hero's current view, at any time. |
| **Help an ally** | Explain who you want to help and how. |
| **Ask Raphael** | Receive a private nudge when you need guidance. |
| **My hero** | Check your condition, use an ability, or manage an item. |
| **Journal** | Revisit discoveries and promises or save a private note. |
| **Pause play** | Stop play whenever you need a break. |

Tell Raphael what you intend and what you hope to accomplish: “I inspect the lantern's painted wing without tearing it. I want to understand the markings.”

If the action has a meaningful cost or uncertainty, Raphael shows his interpretation first. **Confirm action**, **Change my approach**, or **Cancel action**. When a check is needed, press **Roll dice**. The game handles the calculation and saves the result. During a turn-based encounter, your turn card also lets you move, use an ability, or end your turn.

## Guidance, privacy, and breaks

**Show what I see** is also available from the pinned entry. You can use it during another player's turn, combat, a conversation, travel, downtime or a pause. It spends no action or resources and advances no game time. The image arrives privately; **Focus on something** offers a closer view of a listed visible subject. This illustrates what you can already see. Use **Look closer** when you want to investigate something new.

You can keep playing while the image is prepared. **Refresh** checks the same request. If the scene changed, the result says it depicts the earlier view; **Show what I see** requests the current one. In the browser game, the same button is always in the header. **Browser access** gives you a private code to connect your view there. The host must first connect image generation and supply the current scene.

Ask **Distance & reach** in Discord, or **How far? Can I reach it?** in the browser. Choose your character and a visible target or map coordinate. You get the distance in feet, movement needed along a known route, and whether your configured weapon can reach from where you stand. Remaining movement, obstacles, your character's size and turn availability are checked separately. Questions are free even during pauses or other players' turns; they never move or attack for you. The answer uses the current tactical map, so the host must prepare that map and your character first. Ask the host about spells, jumps or other abilities that are not configured. Artwork is not a scale map.

Raphael's counsel is yours until you choose to share it. **Share with the party** previews the exact hint; **Share this hint** publishes it. He points toward possibilities without choosing for you.

Anyone participating may pause without explaining why. When everyone is ready, use **Ready to resume**. Quiet players are never counted as agreeing.

## Come back to the same world

Your completed actions, rolls, promises, and consequences are saved. The world does not advance while you are away.

Use **Resume adventure** or **Gather to resume** to return. An old card may direct you to the current scene; it will not repeat a completed action. If a recap seems wrong, use **Flag a correction** during the debrief.

When in doubt, press **Do something else** and explain what you want. The buttons are invitations, not the limits of your imagination.
