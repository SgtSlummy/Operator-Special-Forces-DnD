## Accepted presentation and interaction

- Latest bounded UI pass uses an approximately 1.618:1 map/right-column split at wide widths, equal columns at 700–999px, and a single column below 700px. The recorded action is a clearly labeled non-interactive badge; outcomes are short and rolls are one compact wrapping line. Optional scene prose lives behind Scene notes; full roll metadata remains in diagnostics.
- The current action title comes before the medium scene art at the top of the right column; the result and roll remain readable. Scene art uses a 16:9 frame, maximum 300px height, and `object-fit: contain` to preserve the full artwork. Movement copy is compact. Keep the map enlargeable and tactical details behind accessible disclosure/select controls.
- Use dark ink `#101b20`, warm ivory `#eee8db`, muted teal `#83b7b1`, and bronze `#c9a775`; serif headings with system-sans body text. Preserve visible keyboard focus, responsive 320/736/1024 layouts, and at least 44px control targets. Do not reopen the settled design direction during a bounded follow-up.
- Previous/Next and history selection navigate recorded moments only. The saved game-action menu is informational, not a row of working game controls.
- Drafts stay in local browser storage. Save/copy does not send, accept, adjudicate, or change a game action. There is no real game-input endpoint, microphone recording, or speech transcription. Device dictation and pasted transcripts are explained honestly as ways to prepare a local draft.
