# The Lanterns of Greyharbor

Every evening, Greyharbor lights its lanterns for those who have not come home.

You used to think it was only a custom.

Then you noticed the empty chairs. A shopkeeper setting out two cups and wondering why. A coat nobody claimed. A mother humming a lullaby in a house she insisted had never held a child.

When you asked about it, a voice answered from a place no voice should have been.

"You remember. Good. Begin there."

His name is Raphael.

## Your place in the world

You live in Avarra, in the river country called the Lantern Reach. Greyharbor is your home: a busy port of flood stairs, ferry bells, old magic, and neighbors who argue because they expect to see one another again tomorrow.

Six ancient beacons protect the Reach. Their light lends something of itself to the lamps in ordinary homes. People say the beacons help travelers find their way back. Recently, that assurance has begun to fail.

You belong to the Lantern Compact, a small fellowship entrusted with missions that cross the boundaries between communities. Your work might be a rescue in a haunted village, a journey through a ruined archive, a negotiation with a rival house, or a stand against something that has stepped through a broken ward.

Your base is the Last Hearth, a former signal house beside the river. There is a room for maps, a kitchen that always seems occupied, and a caretaker named Brother Halden who believes every terrible day can be improved by terrible tea.

## The people around you

The **Hearthbound League** keeps the city supplied and repaired. The **Riverfolk Council** speaks for settlements that value their independence. The **Nightglass Court** offers resources and opportunities that usually arrive with a price.

Lysa tends the civic lanterns. Oren Vale keeps records at the Sunken Archive. Sable brings invitations from the Court. They each have lives, loyalties, and problems that continue while you are away.

People will remember how you treat them. Saving a place does not make it yours. A rival can become a friend. A friend can disagree with you.

## Raphael's chosen

Raphael chose you after a moment when another person's fate mattered enough for you to act. You decide what that moment was.

He may reach you through dreams, flame, water, or a voice only you can hear. His counsel is brief. He points toward things worth noticing and asks questions worth answering. He leaves decisions in your hands.

You may question him. You may disagree. Being chosen does not mean you are destined to win, and it does not mean you must walk someone else's road.

## Before the first session

Give your hero a reason to belong to this company, a person or place they hope to protect, and a promise they have not yet fulfilled. Decide what Raphael's presence feels like to them. No particular class, ancestry, or faith is required.

The campaign begins on Day 6 of the renewed charter, before the Festival of Returning Lights. Three requests wait at the Last Hearth: a settlement facing a ruinous debt, a missing record that could clear an ally, and watchkeepers hearing alarms from an empty road.

One festival lantern has already gone dark.

You remember who made it. Nobody else does.
