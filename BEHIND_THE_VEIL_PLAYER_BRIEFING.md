# Behind the Veil

A Nareth expedition for Raphael's chosen.

Across the water from Avarra's familiar roads, Regent Maelor Voss is preparing to send Nareth's army against the Aster Compact, a neighboring coastal alliance. The island's enchanted Veil conceals his preparations and blocks magical contact across its boundary. Mara Venn, a local organizer, has asked for help.

Your company has three leads: stop the Cinderworks from supplying war constructs, break the Black Hounds' organized pursuit, and open the Veil to communication and escape. Your larger purpose is to stop the war. You choose the order, the methods, and the promises you make to the people living here. Captures, bargains, defections and investigation can matter as much as combat.

If you arrive from Greyharbor, you remain the Lantern Compact, traveling under the name Unmarked Company. The Aster Compact is a different alliance; Nareth's Lantern Network is a local aid organization. Your home and its unfinished promises still matter.

## Before departure

Give your hero a reason to accept the expedition, a moment that established trust in a teammate, and someone or something in Nareth they will not abandon. Existing heroes retain their history and equipment. For a new campaign, the default is four level-3 heroes who have worked together for two months. Agree on a rules edition and content preferences before play.

Arrival is expedition Day 1; the invasion is expected at dawn on Day 13. Your accomplishments can change that schedule. Taking time matters in the fiction; taking time to reply in Discord does not.

## What you know about the Veil

Magical messages, remote observation and magical travel cannot normally cross its boundary. Magic on the same side works normally, and physical crossings remain possible. Your contact has arranged a keyed entry channel. Other channels and escape routes can be found or negotiated. A physical courier needs at least two field days for a return journey from a functioning contact point.

Raphael can still offer his brief, personal counsel. He cannot serve as a cross-boundary messenger or give you unlimited access to hidden answers. A sound escape plan is allowed to work; leaving means deciding what to do about unfinished objectives.

## What the scene shows

| Track | At arrival | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Time | Day 1 | Fictional time remaining before the expected invasion. |
| Heat | 1/6 | Effort committed to searching for your party, based on reports. |
| Supplies | 4 party-days | Shared food reserves; hosted meals and food magic help. |
| Lantern Support | 0/3 | The local network's trust and ability to assist. |

High Heat does not mean enemies know your location. Low Support does not mean every resident opposes you. You keep your ordinary equipment; potions and other valuable items are tracked separately.

Use **Look closer**, **Speak**, **Do something else**, **Help an ally**, **Ask Raphael**, and **Journal**. Describe plans in your own words. Before a risky commitment, you should know the intended outcome and foreseeable cost. You can pause play.

## Your arrival

The signal tower goes dark. Your boat reaches Saltglass Shore beneath the ruined Abbey. A towline snaps: the courier is pinned beneath a spar, and an extra supply crate drifts toward the causeway. A patrol's lantern turns toward the sound.

“Abbey,” the courier says. “Ask for Mara.”

You have your packs, your weapons, and each other. The patrol has not found you. What do you do?
