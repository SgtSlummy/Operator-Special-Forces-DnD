## Capacity and journal recovery implementation

Player projections omit unused command receipts and committed-event history from their isolated working copy. They compute the viewer's visible cells once per request and reuse that result for creature visibility; no cache crosses requests, scenes, conditions or revisions. Read-only projections execute under the authority lock without waiting for a Unity frame, while the HTTP listener remains sequential and commands, receipts and uncertain-command resolution retain their existing dispatch. GM journal views retain the exact saved public snapshots. The Node engine client now permits up to 16 MiB only for an explicitly requested GM projection; other responses retain their 2 MiB bound. Authorization remains inside the authority, and oversized responses still fail closed.

On Windows, the store holds an exclusive writer handle on the WAL for its lifetime. After one full validation it can reuse the protected, verified prefix, checking the exact anchored metadata and file length before each load or append. Appends flush before updating the sealed metadata and snapshot. Reopening still validates every historical frame; other operating systems retain full validation on each save. This removes repeated scans of the entire growing journal from normal Windows writes without changing the save format, receipts or recovery validation.

The first real 100-action workload exposed a 20-second launcher timeout during restart. Separate recovery preserved all 100 pending intentions in about 30 seconds. The launcher now allows a bounded 60-second startup window and records startup and total recovery durations. History is not truncated or compacted; larger and longer-lived campaigns still need measured recovery budgets and a reviewed retention/compaction design before unattended operation can be certified.

## Prepared deviations and split parties

The campaign now saves versioned procedures for splitting the party, alternate routes, negotiation, retreat, delay, and unexpected intentions at every authored location. These are verified procedures for evaluating alternatives against current facts. They are not precomputed outcomes, invented locations, automatic victories or an unrestricted world simulation. Supported engine commands still determine movement, time, combat, inventory and mission outcomes.

New campaigns prepare this metadata at creation. Older saves load unchanged; the human GM can explicitly prepare them while decisions are paused. The live revision-79 campaign was first tested on an isolated copy, then prepared at revision 80 after backup. Source and metadata are available while AI inference remains off.

Each private projection uses the acting character's scene, knowledge and personal rulings; the GM can see separate groups. Public projections omit continuity context. Accepted and declined rulings retain their original scene in the durable action history, including private NPC dialogue, and receipt replay cannot add them twice. Projection windows do not delete older history.

The AI adapter requests the acting character's private projection rather than the overall public scene. It checks campaign, character, scene, revision, pending intention and delegation again before dispatch. Evidence contains bounded visible facts, authorized NPC memories and that character's signed relationship score; it excludes GM relationship maps and other groups' private context. Narrative acknowledgement cannot create mechanical effects. Old-scene requests and human takeover invalidate in-flight proposals. This adapter has passed simulated-provider tests; live Obus inference is still an outstanding gate.

NPC recall keeps the latest authorized memory and selects up to two older keyword matches to the current intention, with recent-entry fallback. It scans at most the engine's 500 retained entries and supplies at most three entries of 500 characters each. This is bounded keyword retrieval, not semantic or unlimited recall; a human can still consult the retained history for an unresolved case. The final AI adapter selection passed **49 tests** after this addition.
