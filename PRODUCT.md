# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

D&D players and their DM, playing a continuing campaign through Discord messages, a Discord Activity, and a standalone website.

## Product Purpose

Present the information players need at the moment it matters, with persistent navigation to previously learned information. Combine richly written scene descriptions, striking illustrations, spatial maps, tactical encounters, and private character information.

## Capabilities and Constraints

One private DM thread contains undisclosed preparation; each player has one private thread for their character, shop access and loot. Shared scenes reveal only discovered or learned information. Room and town tactical maps, layered dungeon overviews, room-route diagrams and world maps describe the same locations. Discoveries receive persistent annotations. Enemy health remains unknown until revealed. Raphael is an angel-like in-world companion whose recall is limited to information visible to the requester. Local Stable Diffusion is the preferred art source. Existing live stores and other project work must be preserved.

## Brand Commitments

The Unwritten Coast. Detailed coastal fantasy environments and illustrated characters; no letter-circle substitutes. Readable, deliberately formatted narration and useful controls rather than text dumps. Discord and web share information and visual language, adapting interactions to each platform.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing campaign catalog, discovery state, room artwork and map renderers: campaigns/the-unwritten-coast/discovery-atlas. Authenticated Discord Activity engine: raphael-council/hollow-lantern. Local Stable Diffusion service at loopback port 7860.

## Product Principles

- Disclosure is enforced before rendering, including artwork and health bars.
- Players can revisit discovered information without taking exhaustive notes.
- Private rewards remain private unless their owner shares them.
- Beauty supports legibility and meaningful decisions.

## Open Decisions

The preview uses an isolated copy of existing campaign content until live rollout is verified. Exact combat miniature art direction and any additional model acquisition remain open.
