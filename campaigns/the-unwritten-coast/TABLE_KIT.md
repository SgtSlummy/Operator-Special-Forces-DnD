# The Unwritten Coast — table kit

**GM ONLY**, except individual handouts released at the stated discovery point. This is a human-run adventure supplement. These tables are not an application import schema, automation policy or replacement for the group's rules.

## Rules baseline

Use the table's chosen D&D fifth-edition rules consistently. The default preparation is two to four level-3 characters; later levels follow README.md. Players use complete, approved character sheets and their normal equipment, spells and features. The GM supplies dice and a way to track position and condition.

The [official Playing the Game reference](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/playing-the-game/) was checked on 2026-09-11 for the basic distinction between ability checks, saves and attacks, and for turn-based combat. It governs the edition it describes. Do not silently mix different editions' grappling, hiding, healing or condition rules.

The check targets, progress tracks, creature numbers and navigation items below are original scenario homebrew. No official challenge ratings are assigned. They have not been playtested or mathematically certified as balanced encounters.

## Make a ruling without stopping the conversation

1. Establish the character's intended result and method.
2. Give information the character can plainly perceive. Reading a legible accessible clue does not need a check.
3. If the proposed action succeeds given the available means and time, resolve it. If it cannot work, explain the relevant obstacle before resources are committed.
4. For meaningful uncertainty, state the stakes and use the applicable adopted rule. Suggested scenario DCs are 10 for a forgiving task, 13 for a pressured routine task and 15 for a demanding technical task.
5. Apply the result, including relevant resource use, and offer the next decision. Do not ask for repeated identical checks until a success appears. A changed method, assistance or more time can change the situation.

Skills listed in episodes are examples. Choose the ability, proficiency, tool or feature matching the actual method. A spell is evaluated under its own rules rather than reduced to an unrelated skill check. Social checks do not seize another person's agency.

## Original opposition profiles

Use normal initiative, actions, movement, attacks, saves and conditions from the adopted rules. If a statistic is not specialized below, use its ability modifier with no added proficiency. No profile has unlisted immunity, resistance, legendary action, spell or automatic countermeasure.

### Quay Constable

A human-sized humanoid official or hired guard. AC 12; HP 12; speed 30 ft; initiative +1. Ability scores: STR 12, DEX 12, CON 12, INT 10, WIS 11, CHA 10. Perception +2; passive Perception 12. Understands local languages as appropriate to the individual. No special senses or saving-throw proficiencies.

**Baton:** one melee attack, +3 to hit, reach 5 ft, one target; 1d6 + 1 bludgeoning damage. **Sling:** one ranged attack, +3 to hit, range 30/120 ft, one target; 1d4 + 1 bludgeoning damage. The constable makes only one of these attacks with an Attack action.

**Behavior:** guards people, a boundary or property. Calls for help, seeks cover, accepts valid instructions and attempts to disengage or surrender when reduced to 6 HP or when its objective is no longer achievable. This is behavior, not automatic immunity to opportunity attacks. Use the adopted rules if a combatant tries to incapacitate without killing.

### Engine Sentinel

A construct protecting machinery. Light and Standard models are Medium; Heavy is Large. Ability scores: STR 16, DEX 10, CON 14, INT 4, WIS 10, CHA 3. Speed 25 ft; initiative +0; ordinary sight and hearing, passive Perception 10. Understands simple local maintenance commands but cannot converse. No special saving-throw proficiencies, resistances or immunities.

| Model | AC | HP | Slam attack | Coil save / damage | Intended campaign band |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|---|
| Light | 13 | 22 | +4 to hit; reach 5 ft; 1d6 + 2 bludgeoning | DC 12 / 1d6 lightning | Levels 3–4 |
| Standard | 14 | 40 | +5 to hit; reach 5 ft; 1d8 + 3 bludgeoning | DC 13 / 2d6 lightning | Levels 5–6 |
| Heavy | 15 | 65 | +6 to hit; reach 5 ft; 1d10 + 3 bludgeoning | DC 14 / 2d6 lightning | Levels 6–7, optional demanding encounter |

**Slam action:** Light and Standard make one slam attack against one target. Heavy makes two separate slam attacks, each with the listed damage. Heavy's two attacks are not two turns.

**Coil:** instead of attacking, the sentinel spends one action visibly charging, fixes a 20-ft-long, 5-ft-wide line and emits a rising tone. On its next turn it can use its action to discharge along that same line. Creatures in the line make the listed Dexterity save, taking the listed damage on failure or half on success. Leaving the line or taking total cover protects characters normally. Discharge consumes the charge. It must charge again before another discharge; it cannot charge, slam and discharge in the same action. Interrupted or isolated power removes the charge.

**Behavior:** announces the protected boundary before attacking. Stops pursuing 30 ft beyond it. Accepts a valid maintenance credential or an authorized shutdown of its control feed. It does not recognize a forged credential automatically; use a scenario check when warranted. Once disabled, it remains disabled until someone repairs it through actual play.

### People who are not combat encounters

Mara, Ivo, Ada, Nessa, Orrin, Tamsin, Wren and Rook have goals and information, not required boss fights. For an unexpected physical conflict, use a fitting adopted NPC profile or the simple Constable numbers if suitable; do not treat that fallback as a claim about their profession. Determine incapacitation and death with the group's agreed rules. Their information also exists in the series bible's independent evidence sources.

## Conservative party scaling

For two PCs, start with at most one active hostile Sentinel or two Constables. For three to four PCs, an early encounter can use one Light Sentinel and one Constable only when both have a coherent reason to fight, or up to three Constables. Start later encounters with one Standard Sentinel; a Heavy model is an optional stronger obstacle, not a required escalation.

These are initial tuning suggestions, not formal encounter budgets. Check current HP, spell slots, rests, escape routes and player preferences. Choose the initial profile before combat and show visible threats. Do not secretly increase HP, add reinforcements or negate a spell because the PCs are winning. Allied helpers normally handle civilian logistics; do not fill initiative with GM-controlled adventurers unless the players want that arrangement.

If a fight is going badly, preserve established withdrawal, cover, surrender and negotiation opportunities. Lower future encounter pressure openly. If it is easy, allow the earned success. Engineering, social and rescue objectives can still be interesting without heavier damage.

## Session-one local hazard: ferry instability

This four-position tracker is original scenario procedure, separate from initiative and separate from campaign Storm Pressure.

Start at **0: strained but afloat**. Advance one step only when an unresolved problem meets its described fictional trigger. Explain the likely consequence before a risky action. Never advance because a player takes time to speak or asks for clarification.

| Step | Visible state | Trigger to reach it | Response |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | One mooring broken; gangway shifting | Opening situation | Secure another line, board, stabilize the gangway or arrange another landing |
| 1 | Ferry drifts 5 ft; direct gangway unusable | A declared rushed line attempt fails without backup, or crew intentionally releases the remaining line | Use the service pontoon, throw a fresh line or board by an actual ability |
| 2 | Floating cargo obstructs the pontoon | A further unresolved drift event occurs during an attempted landing | Clear cargo, use another boarding point or let the harbor launch collect passengers |
| 3 | Ferry grounds on the mudbank 30 ft away | The company and crew abandon securing or landing it through the described drift | Passengers are stranded, not automatically dead; low-water/launch rescue follows and cargo repair becomes a cost |

A secured line or completed safe landing stops further drift. The tracker does not undo completed work. Moving between adjacent safe zones is not itself a trigger. If combat begins, use rounds; a worsening hazard needs a stated round-based trigger, not one advance per player's turn.

## Campaign fronts

These are visible GM records, not new exhaustion, morale or combat rules.

**Storm Pressure 0–6.** Start at 0 on the first evening. Forecast one advance for each 48 hours of actual fictional time thereafter: 1 persistent gusts; 2 exposed crossings become rough; 3 harbor crews shorten shifts; 4 officials open shelters; 5 the front is visible offshore; 6 the Long Gale arrives. Forecasts are known and updated honestly. Repairs reduce damage and increase options; they do not move the storm backward. An actual weather-changing ability can affect the forecast according to its demonstrated scope. At 6 run the current response; there is no automatic campaign loss.

**Shelter Strain 0–4.** Start at 0, with emergency accommodation for the eleven ferry arrivals for the first day. Advance by one only after a full day of an identified unmet need such as beds, food, sanitation or safe access. At 1, volunteers ask for help; 2, one named service is overloaded; 3, residents organize a protest or relocation; 4, an immediate specific shortage requires action. Supplying the need for a full day reduces strain by one. Do not convert the score into off-screen deaths. Record actual residents and supplies separately.

**Official Scrutiny 0–4.** Start at 0, routine inspection. Advance by one for a specific witnessed incident or documented hostile administrative action: 1 formal inquiry; 2 named permits reviewed; 3 contested access and a hearing date; 4 public confrontation or hearing. A justified published correction, settlement or change of authority can reduce it by one. Keep a record of who knows what. Evidence of wrongdoing is not automatically a crime, and Rook cannot act on private knowledge he never obtained.

**Faction relations −2 to +2.** All four factions start at 0. Change by one after a concrete honored promise, help, betrayal or harm, normally at most once per faction per adventure. Note the reason. −2 means serious distrust, −1 reluctance, 0 undecided, +1 willing cooperation, +2 strong working trust. Scores shape conversations and offers; they never force agreement or override individuals.

## Travel, capacity and resources

The original ferry carries twenty passengers plus its two crew safely. It currently arrives with eleven passengers; the crew are not included in the Bellwether resident ledger. There are sixty other Bellwether residents initially in Stillwater, for seventy-one residents involved at campaign start.

A crossing takes ten minutes each way. Allow forty minutes in total for careful boarding, unloading, checking manifests and clearance. Thus one vessel safely completes one twenty-passenger evacuation round trip per one-hour dawn/dusk window. Additional vessels work in parallel only with independent crews, safe landing space and shelter. Relevant magic may improve the plan through its actual limits. Do not quietly increase capacity after a decision has been made.

Provide a 30-gp-per-PC municipal rescue grant after the opening, payable through Holt or an equivalent charitable collection if the PCs reject official sponsorship. It buys ordinary supplies and route access, not allegiance. Record it once. Later rewards are chiefly negotiated pay, facilities, route access and allies; give ordinary treasure appropriate to the table's advancement and equipment rules. This pack supplies no automatic magic-item progression.

The survey lantern points toward a known Stillwater boundary when lit. Combined with a calibrated chart, it identifies the stable route. It has no combat bonus, special damage or authority to choose a destination for the characters. Oil is ordinary lamp oil; one flask supports one expedition window. A replacement instrument exists at the Observatory and another in Bellwether. Copying a chart takes an hour with suitable materials.
