## Use in play

These are scene illustrations rather than dimensionally exact plans. Incidental decoration in generated artwork does not establish treasure, an extra passage, a clue, a mechanical solution or an interactable object. The catalog and GM adjudication define those facts. The measured tactical maps define room dimensions and token coordinates. Numbered overlays indicate approximate visual interaction locations; their tactical positions are authoritative on the plan.

Hidden-room artwork is withheld entirely by the player server until the room is revealed. Discovery results are presented as authored labels and text after successful interactions; the software does not repaint every illustration after an object changes. Full GM images and maps remain private unless deliberately exported by the GM.
