## Default route and useful detours

| Adventure | Core accomplishment | What it makes possible | If the party skips or resolves it early |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01. The Ferry With No Shore | Respond to the ferry emergency; learn about Bellwether | Survivors, lantern and a reason to investigate | Begin with the aftermath and preserve the actual rescue result |
| 02. Supper for the Missing | Arrange shelter and choose a company charter | Home base, witnesses and local legitimacy | Any viable base and support network can substitute for the Crooked Oar |
| 03. The Auction of Borrowed Roads | Obtain a usable crossing route | Reach Bellwether | Ivo's archive route or the Observatory instrument can replace the auction |
| 04. The Village Under Glass | Contact remaining residents; make a return/evacuation plan | First-hand displacement evidence and local knowledge | Respect a decision to evacuate immediately; later scenes use the people already saved |
| 05. The Saint of Small Repairs | Address Mossmere's conductor problem; discover the bypass | A working outer beacon and an alternative to displacement | Repair crews or the Observatory notebook provide an independent path |
| 06. Knives at the Lantern Feast | Protect or publish evidence; renegotiate faction relationships | Equipment, testimony and allies | Hold a smaller public meeting or direct negotiation if the feast is avoided |
| 07. The Weather Below | Calibrate a bypass or discover the controlled shutoff procedure | Technically grounded final plans | Use a supervised test with Wren if the party already secured lawful access |
| 08. A City Votes in the Rain | Establish who authorizes and supports a response | Crews, civilian evacuation and public accountability | A lost vote does not close the docks or independent community organizing |
| 09. The Night Every Beacon Answers | Restore at least two outer beacons or assemble evacuation capacity | A feasible response to the Long Gale | Count earlier repairs; do not break a beacon again to create a mission |
| 10. The Crossing Ledger | Address people and promises still across the boundary | Informed decisions, evacuation records and a return route | If everyone already left safely, use a salvage and consent-check expedition instead |
| 11. The Last Safe Harbor | Get the chosen plan into operational position | A final decision made with known resources and costs | If the storm arrived earlier, this becomes stabilization after its first impact |
| 12. The Coast We Choose | Resolve the threat and settle responsibility | A complete campaign ending | Finish the ending the party earned; no mandatory sequel cliffhanger |

## Four invariants when adapting the route

**Information survives setbacks.** The clue matrix in the series bible lists independent sources. Visit those sources; do not magically relocate the same clue. Characters receive plain information when they can plainly inspect it.

**Objectives matter more than the listed scene order.** A bypass researched in adventure 3 stays researched. A negotiated alliance stays negotiated unless something in play changes it. Award milestones for equivalent accomplishments.

**Unchosen jobs still have a world response.** Tell players the likely implications of spending several days elsewhere. Resolve what independent people can realistically do, then show the result. Never advance a hidden catastrophe solely because players did not follow a prompt.

**A resolved plot is not restarted.** If Rook loses power, later scenes concern his successor and existing systems. If Wren dies, established engineering records and other technicians remain. If the company turns down all institutional jobs, residents, crews and physical evidence still supply meaningful work.

## Session rhythm

Open with a short recap that players can correct. Ask each for an intention. Frame the situation, then stop narrating. During play, alternate opportunities for the characters rather than requiring equal speech lengths. When the immediate danger ends, make space for one human interaction and a practical next decision.

Close with actual outcomes: location, condition, people helped, resources spent, promises, discovered evidence, faction changes, pressure changes and unresolved choices. Keep conjecture separate. Use the save sheet in TABLE_KIT.md. A successful session may include no fight and may finish ahead of the suggested schedule.

## Stakes without compulsory tragedy

These adventures include storms, displaced residents, injury and institutional abuse. Use the boundaries agreed with the players. Substitute property danger, an evacuation problem or a threatened livelihood where needed. A pause never costs fictional time. NPCs have agency; their consent is not an item awarded for a good roll.
