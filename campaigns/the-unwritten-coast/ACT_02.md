# Act II — The price of shelter

**GM ONLY.** Adventures 5–8. Usually levels 4–5. Advance to 5 after securing the alliance/evidence accomplishment associated with adventure 6, and to 6 after establishing a supported civic response in adventure 8. Equivalent accomplishments count. Use [TABLE_KIT.md](TABLE_KIT.md) and the current campaign record.

## 05. The Saint of Small Repairs

**Opening:** outside Mossmere's workshop, children have placed broken kettles beneath a hand-painted portrait of Wren Sol. Inside, Wren is trying to solder a conductor while avoiding the people who want to thank her.

**Objective:** repair or assess Mossmere's beacon and obtain a credible alternative to displacement. If the company chooses another outer beacon first, use its corresponding repair in adventure 9 and let Mossmere's residents continue their own work.

**Locations:** a busy repair yard, the beacon's dry lower chamber and a flooded cable trench. Residents know the safe raised walkways. Show the water and unstable boards before any crossing risk.

**People and moves:** Wren has done years of real public good and helped design the current engine. Both are true. She wants a functioning conductor and an end to questions she cannot answer comfortably. Mechanic Pell can complete the repair without her if given a conductor sleeve and six hours. The town assembly wants assurances that agreeing to repairs does not surrender control of its beacon.

**Routes:** obtain a sleeve from the Brinewatch warehouse; bargain for one with Tamsin; or fabricate one using the workshop and a day of skilled labor. Supplies and sufficient time solve the basic repair. A rushed installation can use DC 13 appropriate tools; failure costs two more hours and reveals the loose connection before activation. A spell that genuinely repairs the material may solve that part immediately; calibration still follows the actual design.

**Complication:** survey stakes mark a larger footprint around the town. Wren confirms that Mossmere is the next proposed displacement site. She can provide her bypass sketch, confess publicly or ask for time to establish a viable alternative. Players decide what to do with her request. No persuasion roll removes her responsibility.

**Evidence:** the stakes, Holt's available diversion schedule and the destination marking in the Crown system independently identify the threat. Wren's sketch, the Observatory notebook and the Stormvault test can independently establish distributed defense.

**Optional danger:** live current arcs across an exposed section only if work begins without isolating it. Warn first; a DC 12 Dexterity save limits 1d6 lightning damage to half. Turning off the feed removes the hazard. Do not add combat unless an actual opponent has arrived with a reason.

**Aftermath:** successful repair marks Mossmere as available, subject to its residents' agreement. Record who paid and who promised what. Wren can travel with the company as an adviser if invited; she does not choose their actions.

**Next invitation:** the Lantern Feast gathers the people who can fund, staff or obstruct the response. If the group prefers direct meetings, use them instead.

## 06. Knives at the Lantern Feast

**Opening:** at the Crooked Oar, every table carries a different borrowed lantern. Somebody has labeled the soup “officially uncharted.” Ivo laughs too loudly, then asks whether his statement can be read before dessert.

**Objective:** decide how to handle the evidence and negotiate concrete cooperation. The title refers to political hostility; an assassination is not inevitable.

**Locations:** the common-room feast, the upstairs chart room and the rear landing. Invitations go to people the company actually knows. Absent or alienated NPCs send a representative or simply do not attend.

**People and moves:** Ivo brings a signed statement and wants his dependents housed safely before going public. Tamsin offers equipment in return for exclusive route rights. Orrin objects to any agreement made on behalf of his crews without their consent. Rook, or an authorized representative, asks to speak privately before the statement is published.

**Rook's offer:** legal recognition, wages and access if the company delays publication until after the storm. He discloses that the engine is an emergency defense but minimizes the residents' risk. Direct questions backed by evidence can expose that minimization. He accepts proof of a workable alternative, though he wants credit. He is not compelled to confess every fact by one successful check.

**Routes:** publish immediately with witnesses; publish after securing safeguards; negotiate a conditional open-access contract; hold a public engineering test; or refuse the entire arrangement and organize independently. Make proposed terms explicit. Suggested commitments include two Ferrymen crews, a Consortium crane and an Assembly shelter. Record acceptance rather than assuming everyone agrees.

**Complication:** a junior official tries to remove the only public copy of Ivo's statement. If the PCs made copies, the attempt loses its leverage. If noticed, the official retreats or claims it needs certification. They can be confronted, followed, searched under an agreed legal process or simply rendered irrelevant by publication. Use DC 13 observation only for the concealed attempt, not for access to the statement itself. A fight uses Constable profiles and immediate de-escalation opportunities.

**Personal beat:** give each character a voluntary conversation about something beyond the case: a meal, family news, a repaired item or a future plan. Never turn every warm scene into a trap.

**Aftermath:** record public facts, private facts, faction promises and publication conditions. Protecting a witness, securing equivalent evidence or earning workable alliances completes the milestone. A failed feast can still leave direct negotiations open.

**Next invitation:** a controlled Stormvault visit can demonstrate whether the alternative works. A company opposing the engine instead needs the same site to find a safe shutoff.

## 07. The Weather Below

**Dungeon map:** use [The Undertow Works](DUNGEON_ATLAS.md), short route R01 → R04 → R08 → R14. The observation/archive loop and drydock branch are optional discoveries; their rewards can change later plans.

**Opening:** below the Crown Beacon, rain falls upward into a copper funnel. A service gauge shows two coastlines where there should be one.

**Objective:** obtain a calibrated bypass, a verified shutdown procedure, or both. If the company already has public authority and Wren's help, this is a supervised engineering operation rather than a forced infiltration.

**Locations:** the service vestibule, the insulated catwalk and the control gallery. The vestibule holds duplicate maintenance records. The catwalk crosses an active but isolatable channel. The gallery has load gauges, destination plates and a large manual disconnect.

**Routes in:** Holt can issue an escorted permit; Wren has a service credential; a retired technician knows the tide-side maintenance door. Unauthorized access can have legal consequences, but none of these routes is artificially closed because the GM prefers a heist.

**Prepared tasks:** read the load diagram; isolate the test loop; record three stable gauge readings; restore the test loop. These are four physical operations, not four mandatory rolls. With instructions, tools and time they succeed. Under an established threat or a rushed deadline, use DC 15 appropriate technical checks for uncertain operations. A failed check triggers a gauge alarm and requires isolating the loop again; it does not silently erase the notes.

**Evidence:** the maintenance register identifies Rook's authorization and Wren's role. The load markings require Crown plus two outer beacons. Test results demonstrate that an inhabited displacement is avoidable when the distributed system has sufficient capacity. The labeled disconnect specifies evacuation before full shutdown; reading its procedure reveals the sequence.

**Optional opposition:** an Engine Sentinel responds to unauthorized access. Use the Standard Sentinel at levels 5–6, or Light at level 4. It announces its boundary and accepts a valid permit or isolated power feed. It does not acquire new immunities to stop player abilities. For two PCs, one sentinel is the default maximum active opponent.

**Aftermath:** record the calibrated readings, copies of the procedure, any equipment damage and who witnessed the test. If the gallery is destroyed, the Observatory notebook and surviving technicians can reconstruct calibration with a day of tests and replacement gauges. Destruction has real cost without making the campaign unsolvable.

**Next invitation:** a civic hearing can authorize the response. Players may bypass politics and build an independent plan, with the practical limits of their actual resources.

## 08. A City Votes in the Rain

**Opening:** the council hall's roof leaks into three buckets. The fourth is being used to collect written questions from the people waiting outside.

**Objective:** establish who supports a response, under what terms, and who gets to decide for the affected communities. A vote can unlock official assets; it does not decide objective engineering truth.

**Locations:** the public hearing, a waterfront logistics yard and the Assembly's open meeting. Each holds a different decision: authority, resources and resident consent. A single speech cannot substitute for all three.

**People and positions:** Holt favors a plan with safe movement and named responsibilities. Orrin needs equipment and realistic crew rotations. The Assembly requires access to information and a voice for those in Stillwater. Tamsin wants trade reopened eventually and can accept a transparent paid contract. Rook argues that existing defense must remain until an alternative is demonstrated.

**Resolution:** list each undecided stakeholder's specific concern. A practical, verifiable answer resolves that concern. Use at most one uncertain social check per distinct unresolved request, usually DC 13 or 15 depending on the actual ask. Do not average a string of rolls into a predetermined verdict. An honest admission of uncertainty can support a pilot scheme.

**Useful evidence:** bring a witness who agrees to attend, demonstrate the calibration, show a copy of the order, or publish a resource budget. Safeguard private addresses and personal details; the public does not need every refugee's history to understand the case.

**If the company wins support:** record specific assets and conditions. Two crew commitments, an accessible shelter and material access are more useful than “everyone trusts us.” If the company loses the vote, the Free Ferrymen, independent repairs and voluntary shelter still function. A later successful test can change minds.

**Aftermath:** resolve Rook's current authority if his conduct was exposed. Preserve any transition already earned. The milestone is a workable supported response or equivalent independent coalition, not winning a particular vote. Reduce Scrutiny if a public settlement addresses its cause; do not erase remaining opponents.

**Next invitation:** the outer beacons need attention, or an evacuation needs boats and beds. Let the party choose the operation matching its actual plan.
