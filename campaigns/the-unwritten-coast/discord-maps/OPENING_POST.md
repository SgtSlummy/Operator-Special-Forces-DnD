## Opening scene

Attachment: campaign-art/unwritten-coast/v1/opening/03-ferry-landing.png

### Brinewatch harbor · The Ferry With No Shore

Rain ticks against the harbor lamps. A small passenger ferry pulls against its mooring ropes, its gangplank trembling above black water.

Eleven exhausted people wait aboard. One keeps asking whether this is Brinewatch. Another holds a bundle of damp street maps against their chest. Every map shows the same hometown. The harbor clerk, standing beneath the office awning, insists no such place exists.

For the moment, the problem is smaller and more urgent: eleven people need to get safely ashore, out of the rain, and somewhere they can be heard.

**Introduce your character and say what you do first.** You might secure the crossing, help the passengers, speak to the clerk, inspect the ferry, or take another approach. No action is chosen for you.

Before the first check, we will confirm the rules edition, character details and dice policy. The campaign's suggested starting level is 3; any change is a table decision.
