# Behind the Veil — Raphael's Nareth expedition

GM-only campaign content and implementation contract.

Adapted from the user-supplied [Campaign Model Writing conversation](https://chatgpt.com/share/6a9ce0e1-bc4c-83e8-8bb1-fc4d07bc22e4). This integration uses the visible conversation. Its downloadable packet was not retrieved; the accompanying JSON is a newly authored blueprint, not that packet or a running rules engine.

## Place in the existing campaign

Nareth is an island province off Avarra's coast. Its regent, Maelor Voss, prepares an invasion of the neighboring Aster Compact, a separate coastal alliance. The **Aster Compact**, the **Lantern Compact**, and **Aster the World Steward** are distinct names and entities. The **Lantern Network** is Nareth's local aid organization, independent of Greyharbor's company.

Run this as an optional expedition after the party establishes itself in Greyharbor, or as a separate campaign using the same Raphael and council framework. In the expedition, the heroes remain the Lantern Compact; “Unmarked Company” is the name used for their visiting party. In standalone play, it is a unit formed two months before arrival. Do not rewrite the established Greyharbor charter or impose a new two-month history on existing characters.

An original bridge scene brings Captain Elian Ward to the Last Hearth with a request from organizer Mara Venn: protect Nareth's residents and stop Voss's war. Ward offers three leads and a means of entry, not authority over the heroes. They may refuse, delay, negotiate terms, or propose another mission. Refusing does not silently start a deadline in an unselected campaign.

Nareth's Veil is a manufactured ward, distinct from the Concord of Witnesses, its six beacons, and the Pale Between. The Hollow Choir is a ritual institution, not a group of Greyharbor's predatory Hollows. Its name does not make its ritualists monsters.

## Setup and clock alignment

Default: four heroes, adaptable to three to five; level 3 through approximately level 8; roughly 10–14 sessions. Existing heroes retain their levels and equipment, and encounters must be prepared for them. Select one rules edition before play; these tracks are homebrew campaign procedures.

Each hero supplies a reason for joining, a trusted teammate and the event behind that trust, and a person or promise in Nareth they will protect. Keep undisclosed personal stakes private.

Arrival is **expedition Day 1**. The invasion begins at dawn on **expedition Day 13**, subject to earned delays. Record the actual Avarra arrival date separately. Expedition day D corresponds to arrival date plus D minus one elapsed days. Never overwrite Greyharbor's Day 6 opening with Nareth's clock. A campaign that has not entered this arc has no active Nareth timer.

## Isolation and Raphael

The Veil blocks magical messages, remote magical observation, teleportation, and planar transit across its boundary. Within either side, ordinary magic functions normally. Physical crossings remain possible. Keyed channels permit specific crossings; an inside contact provides the initial one. A courier's return journey requires at least two field days from a functioning contact point, with an explicit arrival time for any requested help.

Raphael retains his established knowledge of past and present. His brief counsel can reach a chosen hero within Nareth as an explicit setting exception, disclosed before play. It cannot relay messages between separated people across the Veil, reveal undiscovered secrets on demand, or summon outside support. Apply the existing counsel and knowledge boundaries. This preserves his role without making the communication objective meaningless.

A workable escape succeeds under the selected rules and established fiction. Unfinished objectives remain unresolved; retreat never requires permission from the campaign title.

Mara Venn organizes the Lantern Network and prioritizes civilian survival. Captain Rusk of the Black Hounds can reconsider his loyalty when shown evidence that Voss will sacrifice his troops. Engineer Ilyan Sere wants the other bound ritualists freed along with them. Captain Elian Ward sincerely wants to stop the invasion and can advocate for the party. Voss manufactures threats to retain emergency power; ending his command can resolve the war without requiring his death.

**Private truth:** some Aster Compact leaders plan to annex Nareth after Voss falls. Ward is not automatically complicit. Seed three independent discoveries: a draft occupation proclamation, orders assigning a governor's staff, and a diplomatic letter at Crownfast. These are facts awaiting discovery, not a compulsory late twist. Residents have varied loyalties; network Support never represents unanimous public opinion.

## Public tracks and resolution rules

| Track | Initial state | Procedure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Expedition time | Day 1; invasion dawn Day 13 | Count actual fictional time once. Several nearby scenes can share a day. A mission and its night's rest do not automatically cost separate days. |
| Heat | 1 of 6 | Regional enemy effort based on delivered reports, not all-knowing pursuit. |
| Supplies | 4 party-days | One unit feeds the current party for a day. Meals and food magic offset consumption; equipment remains separate. |
| Lantern Support | 0 of 3 | This network's trust and ability to help; award for distinct protective outcomes. |

Time never advances for player response delays, message counts, menu browsing, or a paused session. Rest uses the selected edition's rules. Confirm the known duration of travel or a substantial commitment before resolution.

At operation end, apply the largest newly delivered report: no usable report +0 Heat; credible interference without verified identity +1; publicly witnessed strike or verified identification +2. Do not stack reports from the same incident. Undelivered reports remain pending and can cause one later adjustment for their incident, using only the difference above any severity already applied. Clamp Heat to 0–6.

Heat 0–1 means routine watches; 2–3 brings investigation around reported incidents; 4–5 brings pursuit of known associates; 6 brings a coordinated operation against a last verified location. Heat does not reveal unknown locations or erase known identities when reduced. A full day laying low or relocating with no new report reduces it by one.

Support 0 means cautious contacts; 1 offers shelter and a guide; 2 offers introductions, cover and meals while hosted; 3 can support evacuation, a distraction or a local council. Knowing exploitation or exposure of the network can reduce Support. Clamp to 0–3; repeated narration of one outcome earns no additional award.

At the invasion deadline, remaining assets enter the war and play shifts toward interception, evacuation, negotiation or resistance. It is not automatic defeat. Delays earned before launch change the scheduled date; after launch, foundry success affects future reinforcements without retroactively undoing the invasion.

## Operations

After the opening, present the three operations together. Players choose order, revisit partial outcomes, or attempt Crownfast early. Before each commitment, prepare travel duration, three approaches, independent essential clues, two exits, visible stakes and the consequences of leaving.

| Operation | Approaches and complication | Full outcome | Partial outcome / failure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **No Banner** | Rescue the courier, recover the optional crate, distract the search, or reach the Abbey. Normal equipment is retained. Courier, waterproof map and pilgrim trail independently lead to shelter. Exits: Abbey trail or Reedmarket marsh route. | Durable foothold and contact; Support +1 once. Optional crate adds 2 Supplies once. | Temporary shelter leaves the foothold unresolved. An unsafe landing redirects play toward marsh escape or Reedmarket. Losing the crate never invalidates the foothold. |
| **The Cinderworks** | Worker introduction, maintenance passage, or engineer audience. The oath-core endangers nearby workers. Shutdown, negotiated stoppage or evacuation before destruction are valid. Worker testimony, an engineer and a shipment docket reveal production. Exits: worker ferry or service path. | Remove construct reinforcements; earn a total two-day pre-launch delay. Protecting workers can earn Support once. | Partial: reduced constructs and a total one-day delay. Later full success adds only the remaining day. Failure leaves production active; engineer or shipment evidence offers a new lead. |
| **The Hounds' Ledger** | Take records, negotiate with Rusk, expose orders or confront command. Ledger, clerk and courier orders are independent evidence. Exits: negotiated withdrawal or archive service route. | End coordinated elite pursuit, remove elite reinforcements, reduce Heat by one once. Defection, capture, withdrawal and fragmentation count. | Partial: one officer and patrol stand down; route or credentials gained but main unit active. Failure preserves the Hounds, who adapt only to observed information. |
| **The Hollow Choir** | Sanctuary negotiation, maintenance procession, old gallery or confrontation. Free Ilyan and the other ritualists from their oaths. Engineer testimony, ritual inscriptions and a maintenance record explain control. Exits: pilgrim route or an earned channel. | Disable the Veil or transfer channels to negotiated local authority; open communication and transit and an extraction route. | Partial: one ritualist freed and one token usable for one message OR crossing. Failure moves a ritualist and portable seal to Crownfast, preserving another opportunity. |
| **The Crown Falls** | Evidence, defectors, surrender, political transfer or a war-chamber confrontation. Couriers, public orders and command records expose who can enforce Voss's orders. Exits: physical withdrawal or an earned channel. | End the invasion command and resolve authority over the army. Inherit actual surviving constructs, Hounds, Veil access and local support. | A contested settlement or ongoing war continues into evacuation, negotiation or resistance; do not erase prior gains. |

Do not replace removed reinforcement packages with equivalent enemies. A failed check normally creates one proportionate consequence, not automatic damage plus Heat plus Supplies plus days. Announce risks, let competent plans work, and accept approaches outside this table.

## Opening scene and milestones

The signal tower dims. A snapped towline pins the courier beneath a spar while a reserve crate drifts toward the causeway. The courier asks for Mara at the Abbey. The heroes retain their packs and weapons, and the patrol has not found them. Ask what they do.

Show a local Search clock at 0/3. Advance for ten minutes spent at the landing or a new audible disturbance. Reaching three brings investigation at the causeway, not automatic discovery and not automatic regional Heat. Parallel actions are possible. The source suggests DC 13 Strength (Athletics) for a rushed lift; a lever and coordinated effort may make a roll unnecessary. Adapt checks to the chosen rules and actual approach.

For a new level-3 party, award one milestone for the foothold, each of three distinctly resolved strategic operations, and the war objective: five possible awards, ending at level 8. Record awards once. Define whether a proposed partial settlement meaningfully resolves an operation before awarding it; an upgrade never awards that operation again. Existing campaigns use their established advancement policy, avoiding duplicate rewards for the same arc.

Endings include locally governed Nareth, annexation despite military success, a negotiated fragile peace, or survival into the opening war. Connect epilogues to each hero's reason, bond and stake, and bring surviving promises back to the Last Hearth.

## State, knowledge and Discord integration

[campaigns/behind-the-veil.json](campaigns/behind-the-veil.json) supplies initial content and intended reducer contracts. It is GM-only and must never be bundled wholesale into a browser or Discord payload. It does not implement persistence, validation, AI, encounter balance or a Discord adapter.

Keep GM truth, party-shared knowledge, private character knowledge, and each enemy's knowledge separate. An enemy report needs an observer, claim, recipient, observation time and delivery time. Narrator access to a secret grants no NPC knowledge of it.

Use the existing campaign-scoped append-only events and projections. Every committed action needs a stable event/idempotency key and expected state revision. Apply state changes and their receipt atomically. Keep once-only awards, consumed channel tokens, field-day food consumption, incident report severity, and invasion launch in saved state. Concurrent or repeated deliveries must not duplicate any of them. Persist arrival date, elapsed time and the adjusted deadline together.

Reuse the current Discord deck: **Look closer** = Observe; **Speak** = Talk; **Do something else** = Act or Plan; **Journal** = Journal. Keep **Ask Raphael**, **Help an ally**, character access and pause available. The live scene displays expedition day, the known invasion deadline, Heat, Supplies and Support; show local Search separately only while relevant. Briefings offer the three strategic leads with equal weight. Debrief shows the actual asset changes and any delay earned, and the finale reads those saved outcomes.

The current renderer and browser prototype still require content loading and authoritative services. A future implementation must verify: duplicate events are harmless; partial-to-full foundry delay totals two days; late success cannot unlaunch the invasion; a single-use channel cannot be spent twice; an undelivered report cannot disclose a safehouse; public payloads exclude the annexation truth until discovered; rest and action do not double-charge a day; pause and asynchronous replies do not advance time; saved outcomes determine the finale.
