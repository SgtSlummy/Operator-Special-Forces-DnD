# Witnesslight coverage of the current campaign world

GM planning and verification record for the request to fill Avarra and its story campaign with images. Read alongside the [collection index](COLLECTION_INDEX.md), [world framework](../../WORLD_STORY_FRAMEWORK.md), and [Nareth GM guide](../../BEHIND_THE_VEIL_GM.md). This document contains spoilers.

The current scope comprises the authored Lanterns of Greyharbor campaign, its optional Behind the Veil expedition, the broader world views already created, the full named recurring cast, and the factions, supernatural subjects and objects that make those stories recognizable. Future player choices remain open. Coverage does not require illustrating every possible outcome in advance.

There are exactly six great Concord beacons. Ember Crossing, Thornwake and Glassmere are named; **three communities remain reserved for discovery** in world framework §6. They are future image subjects when play names and establishes them. Neither Greyharbor's Witness Vault nor household and civic lamps add beacons. New drawings must preserve the written travel relationships and avoid fixing unspecified directions from an artistic overview.

The [existing revision protocol](README.md#updating-art-after-player-choices) covers future changes: read a validated event or session recap; identify what visibly changed and what the viewer knows; inspect the closest reference; preserve established layout and appearances; save a new version with its exact prompt, references and source event; review continuity and revelations before sharing. Earlier versions remain part of campaign history. A proposed branch never becomes a played outcome merely because it has an image.

This prepares ongoing generation when player choices supply new facts. It does not require preselecting rescues, deaths, alliances, a final ruler or a world settlement, and it does not claim an automatic session listener or background generation service.
