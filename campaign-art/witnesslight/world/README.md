## How to use the collection

**Established region** means the location or setting has a basis in the campaign guides. Exact architecture, coastlines, terrain, distances, and arrangements shown in the art are interpretive unless already specified in those guides. This label does not make an image safe to reveal to every player: check the party's current knowledge before showing it.

**New visual concept** means an imagined expansion for future adventures. These images do not establish played history, a definitive world map, confirmed destinations, or events the players must encounter. Names such as “southern drylands” and “eastern grasslands” are working visual concepts, not newly fixed geography.

The world overview is a proposed artistic arrangement of Avarra. Use the campaign's written geography when a travel decision depends on direction or distance.

## Continuity to preserve

- The Lantern Reach lies on the western edge of a broken empire. The River Vey connects mountain settlements, reed villages, Greyharbor, and the coast.
- From Greyharbor, Ember Crossing is north and upriver, about one travel day; Bellweather Weir is east, about one day by boat; Thornwake is west, about two days. Glassmere takes about three days through marshes; its compass direction is unspecified.
- There are exactly **six great Concord beacons**: Ember Crossing, Thornwake, Glassmere, and three unnamed communities reserved for discovery. Greyharbor's shared Witness Vault, civic lamps, and household lanterns are not additional great beacons.
- Nareth is an island off Avarra's coast; the Aster Compact is a neighboring coastal alliance. Their precise arrangement remains interpretive. Nareth's manufactured Veil is distinct from the Concord and Pale Between.
- Images depict places and possibilities. They do not decide a rescue, war, allegiance, ruler, or the party's next move.

## Keep the same art as the story changes

Witnesslight combines ultra-realistic people and materials with dramatic D&D book illustration, a restrained oil-painted finish, strong silhouettes, and selective detail. Warm witness-light contrasts with muted slate and mineral colors; quieter areas remain simple.

For a new scene or revision, use the relevant saved image and prompt in [manifest.json](manifest.json) as the starting point. Record the actual player choice, what visibly changed, what must stay consistent, and who may see the result. Generate a new numbered version, preserve the earlier image and its prompt, and update the asset record and index. Unchosen alternatives remain possibilities rather than campaign history.

Artwork is generated when requested; this collection does not automatically monitor sessions or generate future images.
