01–09 · Join the squad

First-time setup happens once. A returning player skips to the saved session. Character approval and the session start remain GM decisions.

10–16 · Make the mission your own

Short situations lead to open choices. Intent, requested rolls, results, and NPC claims remain distinct. Route preferences support discussion.

17–19 · Combat, when the story calls for it

This branch is optional. It ends when the actual encounter ends. The schematic map conveys known positions only; it is not a rules-valid encounter map.

20–24 · Come home, remember, return

Record what actually happened, settle shared items, play downtime, review the recap, and save a truthful starting point for next time.

25–34 · Keep people moving when something changes

These states have their own screens and next steps. No hidden failure, repeated roll, lost draft, or personal concern should leave a player guessing.
