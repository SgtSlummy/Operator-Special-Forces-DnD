# The Flooded Boathouse — an eight-phase Discord game

A complete visual walkthrough for **Raphael's Council**, with four alternating player spotlight turns between the opening and the debrief.

**Party:** Maren, a ranger and the session host; Rowan, a cleric. **Mission:** bring ferry keeper Elin home before high tide. **Setting:** Greyharbor, Day 6. This is a new illustrative side mission, not a change to the existing campaign's saved history.

Each image shows two moments in one phase. They are separate screen states, not custom side-by-side panels inside Discord. The controls use the established message/button/modal design. Styling is illustrative rather than a pixel-exact Discord screenshot. The bot, dice results, save receipts, and world changes shown here are examples, not a connected or executed session.

## The full turn sequence

| Phase | Who acts | Game time | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 · Join | Both players; host begins | 17:00 | Two approved heroes, both ready. |
| 02 · Agree | Both vote separately | 17:00 → 17:10 | Accept rescue; reach the boathouse. |
| 03 · Turn 1 | Maren | 17:10 → 17:15 | Investigation: 9 + 3 = 12; discover the ward pattern. |
| 04 · Turn 2 | Rowan | 17:15 → 17:20 | Receive/share counsel; speak to Elin; no roll. |
| 05 · Turn 3 | Maren | 17:20 → 17:25 | Confirm assistance; Rowan gains advantage. |
| 06 · Turn 4 | Rowan | 17:25 → 17:30 | Keep 16 from 6 and 16; +2 = 18; free Elin. |
| 07 · Debrief | Company returns; both vote | 17:30 → 17:40 | Elin home; consequences recorded; agree to end. |
| 08 · Save/resume | Both players; host resumes | Remains 17:40 | Same world, fresh readiness check. |

These are narrative spotlight turns, not a combat initiative sequence. Check targets, time costs, and the assistance ruling are chosen for this short demonstration; a live campaign must validate them under its selected rules adapter. There are no omitted enemy turns because this sample is a rescue and investigation, not a battle.

## 01 · Join the company

*Setup — Day 6, 17:00*

Maren joins through a native Discord text-entry form. Rowan follows the same flow for his own hero. Each reviews and approves their validated character draft, and both mark themselves ready. Maren is the session host.

**Button path:** Join the company → type a hero → Submit → My hero → Approve hero draft → I'm ready. The host presses Begin session only after both heroes are approved and both players are ready.

The image shows the joining form and the later ready lobby. It does not mean submitting a character description automatically approves it.

## 02 · Briefing and agreement

*Party decision — 17:00 to 17:10*

Lysa asks the Lantern Compact to bring ferry keeper Elin home from a flooded boathouse before high tide at 18:00. A magical ferry ward seals the door. The briefing discloses ten minutes of travel in each direction.

Maren proposes the mission. Maren and Rowan each independently support the plan: “Go together and bring Elin home.” With two of two votes recorded, either participant can confirm the agreed plan. They reach the boathouse at 17:10.

**Button path:** Missions → open the briefing → Propose this mission → each player presses Support plan → Confirm agreed plan.

The briefing is private browsing. The agreed plan and arrival are shared. The disabled controls in the right-hand image belong to the proposal that has already been committed.

## 03 · Turn 1 — Maren investigates

*Maren's spotlight — 17:10 to 17:15*

Maren presses Look closer and writes: “I study the marks on the boathouse door to understand what is holding it shut.”

Raphael returns an action review and an Investigation check: 1d20 + 3 against a target of 12. Maren confirms and presses Roll dice. The example result is **9 + 3 = 12 — success**.

**Raphael:** “Three lantern marks repeat across the ward. Their pattern can steady its light.”

**Button path:** Look closer → type intention → Submit → Confirm action → Roll dice.

The check reveals a useful pattern, not a complete solution. The rolled check becomes a saved result with its old roll control disabled. Rowan receives the next spotlight.

## 04 · Turn 2 — Rowan seeks guidance and speaks

*Rowan's spotlight — 17:15 to 17:20*

Rowan asks Raphael privately for a nudge. Raphael replies: “Before you ask the door to open, ask who is listening.”

Rowan chooses to share that exact hint. He then presses Speak and writes: “Elin, are you inside?”

**Elin:** “Here! The ward will not release me.”

**Button path:** Ask Raphael → type question → Submit → Share with the party → Share this hint → Speak → type dialogue → Submit.

Reading and sharing counsel do not advance fictional time. The conversation advances this example scene to 17:20. No roll is needed because Elin can hear Rowan and wants to answer. Only the approved hint becomes public; Rowan's private question does not.

## 05 · Turn 3 — Maren helps Rowan

*Maren's spotlight — 17:20 to 17:25*

Maren presses Help an ally and writes: “I hold my lantern beside Rowan's and follow the ward pattern I recognized, helping him steady its light.”

Raphael explains that Maren's earlier discovery makes the assistance effective. The review shows the example ruling: **advantage on Rowan's next Arcana check**, a five-minute cost, and no item consumed. Maren confirms.

**Button path:** Help an ally → describe the assistance → Submit → Confirm action.

**Raphael:** “The lights hold steady. Maren's help is ready for Rowan's next check.”

Maren has committed her own assistance. She has not rolled for Rowan or chosen his action. The right-hand image shows the review after confirmation, followed by the shared result.

## 06 · Turn 4 — Rowan rolls and frees Elin

*Rowan's spotlight — 17:25 to 17:30*

Rowan describes his intention: “I calm the ward and open a way for Elin.” After his action is reviewed and confirmed, Raphael presents the requested Arcana check.

The example uses advantage from Maren's help: roll two d20s, keep the higher, then add Rowan's +2 modifier. The target is 13. Failure would leave the ward closed and cost five minutes; the party could choose another approach instead of being forced into a dead end.

**Example roll:** 6 and 16 → keep 16 → add 2 → **18 — success**.

**Button path:** Do something else → describe the action → Submit → Confirm action → Roll dice.

**Raphael:** “The ward opens. Elin steps safely outside.”

This successful attempt takes five minutes. The check's result is saved once. Its disabled button communicates completion; an eventual server implementation must also prevent duplicate resolution independently of the button's appearance.

## 07 · Debrief — the world remembers

*Return and party decision — 17:30 to 17:40*

The company escorts Elin home under the already-agreed rescue plan. The return journey takes ten minutes. At 17:40, Raphael gives the debrief:

- Elin is safely home before the 18:00 tide.
- Forty minutes have passed since the mission began, including both journeys.
- No items were consumed and nobody was injured.
- Elin's trust in the company has improved.
- Repairing the unstable ferry ward remains an open lead, not an automatically accepted mission.

**Button path:** Continue scene → read the debrief → optionally Read the record or Flag a correction → Propose session end → each player presses Support plan → Confirm agreed plan.

The right-hand image shows the final session-end decision before confirmation. Viewing the debrief does not apply the consequences again.

## 08 · Save and resume

*Session end — Day 6, 17:40; later real-world return*

Raphael presents the last saved place, game time, completed rescue, and remaining lead. The example save receipt is GH-S01.

Days later in real life, the party returns to **Day 6, 17:40**, with Elin still safely home and the ward repair still unresolved. Neither player is automatically marked ready.

**Button path:** Gather to resume → each player presses I'm ready → the host presses Begin session once both are ready.

The right-hand image deliberately shows zero of two ready and a disabled Begin session button. No offline world time has elapsed.

## Files and implementation notes

All eight PNGs are saved beside this guide. The [generation prompt specification](generation-prompts.json) contains the common brief, phase prompts, and the two targeted correction prompts. The images were generated with the built-in image-generation tool and visually checked for turn ownership, vote agreement, private/shared boundaries, arithmetic, and continuity of the game clock.

The picture sequence shows buttons, private replies, and modal text entry; it does not add a custom dashboard to the Discord client. A pinned entry is retrieved through Discord's Pins menu, not permanently fixed above the chat. The component definitions and event-handling requirements remain in the [Discord deck design](../../DISCORD_PLAY_DECK.md) and [renderer integration notes](../../raphael-council/discord/README.md).

The sample's persistence is a design requirement. A production bot must authenticate the player, enforce ownership and game state, commit each result once, and restore it from a durable database. These images do not implement those services.
